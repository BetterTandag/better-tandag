# BetterTandag Code of Conduct

**By participating in BetterTandag.org, you agree to abide by this Code of Conduct.**

This is a civic project. The people it serves are residents of Tandag looking for a service, a hotline, or a
figure they can trust. Everything below follows from that.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all.

We are committed to fostering an environment that respects and promotes the dignity, rights, and contributions
of all individuals, regardless of characteristics including race, ethnicity, caste, colour, age, physical
characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation,
language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socio-economic position, level of education, or
other status. The same respect is owed to every language spoken here — English, Filipino, Surigaonon,
Kamayo — and to contributors writing in a second or third language.

## Encouraged Behaviors

We believe that a healthy community depends on more than the absence of harm. Every participant agrees to:

1. **Respect the purpose of the community**, its activities, and the ways people gather.
2. **Engage kindly and honestly** with others.
3. **Respect different viewpoints and experiences.**
4. **Take responsibility** for your actions and contributions.
5. **Gracefully give and accept constructive feedback.**
6. **Commit to repairing harm** when it occurs.
7. **Behave in other ways that promote and sustain the well-being of the community.**

## Restricted Behaviors

The following are not acceptable in any project space:

1. **Harassment.** Violating explicitly expressed boundaries, or engaging in unnecessary personal attention
   after a request to stop.
2. **Character attacks.** Making insulting, demeaning, or pejorative comments directed at a community member
   or group of people.
3. **Stereotyping or discrimination.** Characterising anyone's personality or behaviour on the basis of
   immutable identities or traits.
4. **Sexualisation.** Behaving in a way that would generally be considered inappropriately intimate in the
   context, or otherwise objectifying a person.
5. **Violating confidentiality.** Sharing or acting on someone's personal or private information without
   their permission.
6. **Endangerment.** Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm toward any person or group.
7. Behaving in other ways that threaten the well-being of the community.

### Other Restrictions

1. **Misleading identity.** Impersonating someone else for any reason, or pretending to be someone else to
   evade enforcement actions.
2. **Failing to credit sources.** Not properly crediting the sources of content you contribute.
3. **Promotional materials.** Sharing marketing or other commercial content in a way that is outside the norms
   of the community.
4. **Irresponsible communication.** Failing to responsibly present content which includes, links to, or
   describes any other restricted behaviours.

## Accuracy is a conduct issue, not just a quality issue

This section is specific to this project, and it is not decoration.

Most projects treat wrong data as a bug. Here it is closer to a harm. A wrong hotline is dialled in an
emergency. A wrong permit requirement costs someone a trip to City Hall and a day's wage. A wrong figure on a
transparency page gets quoted back at the people it was meant to hold to account.

So **knowingly contributing a number, a date, a requirement, or a contact you have not verified — or presenting
a guess as sourced — is treated as a Code of Conduct matter**, not a code-review note. This is the same
principle as "failing to credit sources" above, applied to civic data.

Saying "I am not sure" is always welcome and never penalised. Guessing quietly is the problem.

Two related rules:

- **A person's name is data, not prose.** Names of officials, staff and residents belong in `content/` and
  `config/lgu.config.json`, where each carries its source and check date. In a document, a comment, an issue
  or a commit message they are unsourced claims about real people — refer to the role instead.
- **This project is not an official publication of the City Government of Tandag**, and no participant may
  present it as one.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially
representing the community in public or other spaces.

Today that means **GitHub only** — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and code review.

The project has **no Discord, no social media accounts, and no published contact address.** If you are
directed to a channel claiming to be an official BetterTandag space, treat it with suspicion and raise it on
GitHub.

## Reporting an Issue

Tensions can occur between community members even when they are trying their best to collaborate. Not every
conflict represents a violation of this Code of Conduct, but it is important to report an incident promptly.

**To report a concern, contact a moderator** — a maintainer of this repository — and they will look into it.

Moderators will investigate by reviewing the relevant issues, pull requests, and messages, and by speaking with
those involved. They will strive to be transparent about the process while prioritising the safety and
confidentiality of the person reporting. A moderator who is themselves involved in an incident will recuse
themselves from handling it.

## Addressing and Repairing Harm

Moderators may take any action they judge proportionate, guided by this ladder:

1. **Warning** — for a single incident, or a first-time occurrence.
   _Repair:_ a private written warning setting out what happened and what is expected instead, together with
   an apology or acknowledgement where one is owed.
2. **Temporarily Limited Activities** — for a repeated violation, or a first-time incident of greater
   severity.
   _Repair:_ a time-limited restriction from some project spaces or activities, so that everyone has room to
   cool down.
3. **Temporary Suspension** — for a pattern of repeated violations, or a single serious violation.
   _Repair:_ a time-limited suspension from all project spaces, with the conditions for returning stated
   plainly at the outset.
4. **Permanent Ban** — for repeated violations that other measures have not resolved, or a violation so
   serious that the community's safety requires removal.
   _Repair:_ none. This step exists to protect the community.

Contributions containing deliberately false civic data are reverted regardless of which step applies.
